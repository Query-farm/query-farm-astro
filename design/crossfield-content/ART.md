# Crossfield visual direction

The public overview explains decisions and outcomes before implementation. SQL
remains a verifiable interface example, revealed when the reader wants to inspect
the mechanism. Documentation keeps runnable code and setup.

## Chapter openings

The overview omits repeated eyebrow labels above section headings. Three major
chapters use `ChapterOrnament.astro`: a native SVG of green field contours and an
ochre parcel, echoing the illustration series. Other headings stand alone. The
ornament is decorative and hidden from assistive technology; headings and the
chapter navigation provide the structure. No raster generation is involved.
The ornament spans its full content column on desktop and phones, with a fixed
shallow height and non-scaling strokes.

## Shared ground

Asset: `public/crossfield/art/shared-ground.webp`. Generated with the built-in
image-generation tool; converted to WebP without cropping for delivery.
The original generated PNG is retained at the tool's saved location. This is
editorial artwork, not a diagram of execution or a data visualization.

Final generation prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial abstract artwork for the Crossfield product homepage at Query Farm. Create one refined landscape-format abstract art piece, no text anywhere. Subject: distinct cultivated fields seen from above becoming a coherent woven pattern, an expressive metaphor for giving separate data sources a shared business meaning while keeping their identities. Use restrained large geometric curved strips and rectangular terraced parcels, crop-row lines, two or three offset translucent paper planes, a small warm sun disc. Composition: centered, balanced but asymmetrical, generous warm ivory negative space at the outer edges; suitable alongside headline text, not behind it. Medium: sophisticated hand-pulled screenprint and cut-paper collage on lightly textured natural stock, slight ink grain, crisp deliberate shapes, matte surface. Palette exactly warm ivory #f7f3e8, field green #46632f, sage #a5b090, golden ochre #d9a441, clay #a97c50, dark brown #3c3021. The visual should feel like an architectural landscape abstraction, credible editorial design for a technical product. Avoid photorealism, literal farm buildings, cows, people, robots, computers, database cylinders, dashboard UI, charts, lettering, logos, gradients, neon glow, excessive tiny detail, arrows. Landscape aspect approximately 4:3.

Diagrams are authored in accessible HTML/CSS/SVG, with captions distinguishing
illustrative numbers from the separately verified retail fixture. They do not
encode essential labels into the generated bitmap.
