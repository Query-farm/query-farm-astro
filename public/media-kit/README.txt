One-liner:

Short (~50 words):
Query.Farm gives DuckDB three practical paths: extend it with open-source

Long (~150 words):
Query.Farm gives DuckDB — and, increasingly, other query engines — three
practical paths: extend it with open-source DuckDB extensions, connect it
to APIs, files, and other systems through VGI (Vector Gateway Interface),
and operate it in production with Haybarn, a signed and hardened
distribution. Rowfence adds row- and column-level security to VGI, Cupola
is a desktop client for querying with DuckDB and Haybarn, and Orchard is a
growing marketplace of ready-made VGI connectors.

Founded by Rusty Conover — who has contributed to DuckDB's core, and
previously held senior engineering and CTO roles at J.P. Morgan, Two
Sigma, and Schonfeld Strategic Advisors — Query.Farm shipped its first
extension in May 2024. Since then it has published 32 open-source DuckDB
extensions, used in more than 229M+ loads a year, shipped VGI worker SDKs
in 6 languages, and contributed 64 merged pull requests directly to
DuckDB's core.

(Figures above are a snapshot at the date on this file. For current

NAME & TRADEMARK
----------------
In running text, write it exactly like the wordmark: Query.Farm — capital
Q, capital F, the dot kept, no space. Lowercase ("query.farm") is fine for
a URL, a badge, or code — never mid-sentence.

  Correct:    Query.Farm
  Incorrect:  QueryFarm
  Incorrect:  Query Farm

Query.Farm and the Strata Sun mark (the banded circle) are trademarks of
Query Farm LLC. You're welcome to use them to refer to us — link to a
product, credit an integration, embed a badge — but please don't recolor
or distort the mark, and don't use it in a way that implies our
endorsement or a partnership that doesn't exist. Ask first if you're not
sure: hello@query.farm.

Brand name vs. legal name: "Query.Farm" (with the dot) is the brand — what
goes in the wordmark, the product UI, and your article. The registered
legal entity is "Query Farm LLC" — no dot, because an LLC's legal name
can't contain punctuation like a period. Use "Query Farm LLC" in a
contract, invoice, or copyright line; use "Query.Farm" everywhere else.
